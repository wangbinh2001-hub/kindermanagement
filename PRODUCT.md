# Product Truth: KinderManagement

## Audience
- **School Administrators:** Manage classes, teachers, student enrollments, and tuition fees.
- **Teachers:** Manage attendance, academic records, and communicate with parents.
- **Parents:** Track their children's progress, attendance, and pay tuition fees.

## Purpose
A multi-tenant Kindergarten Management System that simplifies school operations and improves communication between the school and parents.

## Constraints
- Must be a high-performance, responsive web application.
- Must support multi-tenancy (each school has isolated data).
- Data privacy and secure authentication (Supabase).
- Localized in Vietnamese.

## Voice
- Professional, trustworthy, and clear.
- Welcoming and modern.
